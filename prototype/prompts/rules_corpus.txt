# Rules Induction Prompt (Ontology-Aligned)

**Role:** Knowledge Engineer and Educational Data Scientist
**Goal:** Derive symbolic rules from the corpus that capture key relationships between concepts in the educational ontology.

---

## Instructions:

1. Analyze the corpus to identify key educational concepts and their relationships.
2. Create symbolic IF-THEN rules that capture:
   - Prerequisite relationships (concept A is required before concept B)
   - Dependency relationships (mastery of concept A affects understanding of concept B)
   - Pedagogical insights (teaching approach X is effective for concept Y)
   - Assessment relationships (concept A can be assessed through activity B)

3. For each rule:
   - Use concepts that would likely appear in the ontology
   - Include a confidence score (0-100%) reflecting certainty
   - Provide a brief justification that references the ontology structure
   - Make sure rules align with the ontology hierarchy and properties

## Output Format

```
Rule ID: [Incremental number]
IF: [Condition statement using ontology concepts]
THEN: [Consequence statement using ontology concepts]
Confidence: [0-100]%
Justification: [Brief explanation with reference to ontology structure]
Ontology Classes: [Classes from the ontology involved in this rule]
```

Ensure that rules are derived from evidence in the corpus and would align with an educational ontology.
